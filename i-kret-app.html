Critical — chest pain, breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, severe bleeding, stroke signs → redirected to Emergency, not the OP queue.
High — high fever, vomiting, severe pain, diabetes-related symptoms → prioritized ahead of routine visits.
Moderate — persistent but non-acute symptoms.
Low — routine checkups, follow-ups, mild symptoms.
